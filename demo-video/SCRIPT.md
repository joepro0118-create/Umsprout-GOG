**On-screen caption:**
> One missed clause can disqualify a Malaysian SME tender bid.

**Voiceover (29 words):**
Malaysian SME contractors spend days preparing bids for public tenders. Many documents mix Bahasa Malaysia and English across dozens of pages. Missing one required code can trigger immediate rejection.

**On-screen caption:**
> A preliminary readiness assessment against your company profile, with citations.

**Voiceover (28 words):**
TenderMate gives a preliminary readiness assessment before you bid. It extracts tender clauses, checks structured rules against your company profile, and links each item to its source page.

## Scene 8 — Close (1:45–2:00, 15s)

**On-screen caption:**
> Preliminary readiness assessment only. Always verify against official tender documents.

**Voiceover (32 words):**
TenderMate is a preliminary readiness assessment, not legal advice. It can miss clauses, and scanned pages rely on OCR. Always verify every requirement against the official tender documents before submitting your bid.
